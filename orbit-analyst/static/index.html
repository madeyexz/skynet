Working

    How it works

    1 · Read

    It finds the place with OpenStreetMap, the dates in your words, and what you want measured: water, ships or new building.

    2 · Choose

    Colour images are easy to read but clouds block them. Radar sees through cloud and at night. It checks the cloud first, then picks.

    3 · Measure

    It reads Sentinel-1 radar or Sentinel-2 colour images from Planetary Computer and compares before with after, pixel by pixel.

    4 · Answer

    Numbers, a before/after slider, a map, what it cannot see, every source, and a quote for a sharper image if you need one.
